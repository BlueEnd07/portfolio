About

Nice to meet you 🙂

I'm a 3D artist and generalist. By day I make photoreal product renders and animations for smart-home hardware; by night I render coffee cups, perfume bottles and anime characters just because they're fun.

My home base is Blender — modeling, lighting, lookdev, animation — and lately I've been mixing in AI tools: diffusion models, training my own LoRAs, and turning drone footage into 3D scenes with Gaussian splatting.

If it can be made to look real (or delightfully unreal), I'm in.
