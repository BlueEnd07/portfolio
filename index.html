About

Nice to meet you 🙂

I'm a 3D artist who's curious about everything. For the past 2 years I've been a 3D artist at Keus, making product animations and renders for their smart-home products. Outside work I try whatever new thing has caught my attention.

Right now that's Gaussian splatting and digital twins (capturing real places and turning them into explorable 3D), Unreal Engine and real-time work, games, and AI tools: ComfyUI pipelines and training my own LoRAs.

My base is Blender, but I'll learn whatever tool the idea needs.
