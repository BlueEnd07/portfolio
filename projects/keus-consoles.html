In-house · Keus

One tap, and the evening switches on.

A short vertical film for Keus smart-home consoles: a hand presses a scene button and a dark kitchen-dining space lights up layer by layer. Product renders of the console finishes come after it.

Company
Keus (in-house)
Role
3D, lighting, animation
Tools

Output

▶ film

The ideaLight is the product

The console is small, so the film shows what it does: the room changes. Each lighting layer (pendants, cove, under-counter strips) is staged to come on in sequence, so one tap reads as a full mood change.

The productFinishes & packaging

Studio renders of the console range: the Metal Series finishes, backlit buttons, and the finish sampler packaging.
