Zoro Plush

A small toy, captured from every side and turned into a 3D Gaussian splat

After a cycle track and a whole office lobby, something much smaller: a Zoro plush from my desk. Captured all the way around and isolated on black, so you can spin it and get close to the fuzzy hair and the stitching, right here in the browser.

Why an objectSplats up close

Rooms test how far a splat can stretch. A small object tests the opposite: fine detail at close range. Soft fabric and fuzzy hair are hard to model by hand but come out naturally as splats, and a clean, isolated capture like this can be dropped straight into a 3D scene.
