Hands that feel real.

App shots with the phone held and tapped by CG hands made in Blender. Because the hands are 3D, any screen, pose or camera angle can be re-rendered without a photo shoot.

The hard partSkin, nails, contact

Hands look fake when the details are wrong: skin that doesn't scatter light, fingertips that don't press, a grip with no weight. These shots get that contact right, so the screen stays the focus.
