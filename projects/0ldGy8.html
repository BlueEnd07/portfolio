Blender Pills

Published 2025-01-15 · Personal work

The Blender-inspired pill project showcases a unique product concept designed entirely in Blender. It features a set of embossed pills with natural textures, housed in a sleek, branded packaging. This project captures Blender’s essence through creative design, clean modeling, and professional presentation, making it visually appealing and functional.
Process Breakdown
1. Conceptualization
Brainstormed a product concept: a "Blender Pill" as a metaphorical boost for Blender projects.
Incorporated Blender branding, including the logo, typography, and orange-themed color palette.
2. Modeling
Pills:
Created circular pill geometry with smooth edges for a realistic pharmaceutical look.
Added embossed "BLENDER" text for branding.
Applied a speckled texture to mimic a natural, organic material.
Packaging:
Designed a compact, branded box using sharp, clean geometry.
Ensured the packaging dimensions fit multiple pills for a functional look.
3. Texturing
Used procedural textures for the pills to create a natural speckled appearance.
Applied branding elements to the packaging, focusing on bold typography and the Blender logo.
4. Lighting & Composition
Set up soft, diffused lighting to highlight product details and maintain a professional tone.
Included props like a minimalist tray and a small plant to enhance the composition and add realism.
5. Rendering
Rendered the scene with a clean, bright setup to keep the focus on the product.
Used multiple angles to showcase the pills, packaging, and overall aesthetic.
6. Post-Processing
Performed color correction and fine-tuning in Blender’s compositor to enhance vibrancy and sharpness.
Ensured the final renders had a polished and professional finish.
Outcome
Visuals: High-quality renders with a clean, modern look that highlight the embossed pill details and branded packaging.
Branding: A cohesive design that resonates with Blender's identity, using its iconic logo and color scheme.
Presentation: A visually appealing layout with a minimalist yet professional aesthetic, ideal for product showcases or marketing.
This project effectively merges creativity with technical precision, offering a practical and engaging visual representation of the Blender brand. Let me know if you'd like to refine or expand this!
